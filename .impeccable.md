## Design Context

### Users

Texas homeowners with a roof problem, a solar question, or both. They arrive after a trigger: a storm, a high electric bill, an insurance letter, a neighbor's new panels. Often on a phone, often stressed. They want a straight answer and a clear next step.

### Brand Personality

Warm, friendly, simple. The voice is a calm person who does this work every day and explains it plainly. No urgency tricks, no countdown timers, no hype. Honest about limits. Texas-grounded: real places, real weather, real utilities.

### Aesthetic Direction

Reassuring and uncluttered. Generous space, soft edges, clear type hierarchy. The theme already exists in `src/styles/texas-solar-and-roofing-pros-theme.css` and `src/styles/theme.css`; polish aligns to it, it does not replace it. Fonts: Switzer body, Gloock display, JetBrains Mono for numbers, BespokeSerif for large pull quotes. (Corrected 2026-09-14: the earlier `Outfit` note was stale — CLAUDE.md, `src/styles/texas-solar-and-roofing-pros-theme.css` and `public/fonts/` all ship Switzer.) Anti-references: lead-gen landing pages with popups and fake scarcity, generic "AI dashboard" looks.

### Design Principles

1. One idea per screen. Reduce before you add.
2. Numbers are the proof. Show real figures, tabular, easy to scan.
3. Warmth through space and tone, not decoration.
4. Every interactive element has all states and a 44px touch target.
5. Follow the existing tokens. No new hard-coded colors or spacing.

### Quality Bar

Flagship for the public site. Light theme is primary.

**Dark exception — motion work.** Video and motion pieces run dark, on Night Sky
`oklch(10% 0.028 273.77)` with Deep Sky for emphasis bands. This matches the live
`/our-process` page, which is already dark, and gives photography somewhere to sit.
Light stays primary for every page of the site itself.
