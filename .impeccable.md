## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are Central Texas homeowners in two high-intent situations:
- Proactive homeowners who want to cut energy bills, conserve resources, and invest in long-term energy resilience.
- Reactive homeowners navigating storm or damage events who need trustworthy guidance and fast next steps.

They are often comparing multiple providers, want confidence quickly, and need a low-friction path to action.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel modern, relevant, and unique.

Emotional goals:
- Confidence in technical and financial decisions
- Clarity during high-stress repair scenarios
- Momentum toward a concrete next step

### Aesthetic Direction
A contemporary Texas-local service brand with a high-trust, conversion-focused experience.
The interface should balance energy-forward innovation with grounded practical credibility.

### Design Principles
1. Conversion First: Every page should clearly and repeatedly channel users toward completing the contact form.
2. Trust Through Specificity: Replace generic marketing patterns with concrete proof, clear process cues, and transparent language.
3. Dual-Intent Clarity: Support both proactive solar/efficiency seekers and storm/damage responders without muddled messaging.
4. Distinctive Restraint: Use a memorable visual point-of-view that feels modern and unique without gimmicks.
5. Frictionless Action: Make contact CTAs persistent, obvious, and fast across device sizes.
