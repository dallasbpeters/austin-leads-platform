## 9. FAQ

**Section headline**
Common questions

**When do you start taking bookings?**
We open the booking calendar in the spring. Install windows start filling in July, so booking early gives you the most flexibility on dates.

**Do I need to supply the lights?**
No. Lights are included in all packages. We use commercial-grade LEDs that we own, maintain, and replace as needed. If you have existing lights you'd like us to install instead, let us know when you request a quote and we'll confirm compatibility.

**Do I need to be home during the install?**
No. We just need clear access to the front of the property. We'll confirm the install window by text the day before and send a photo when the job is complete.

**What if something stops working after install?**
Mid-season service is included on all packages. If a section goes dark, contact us and we'll schedule a service visit. Most issues are a bulb or a loose connection and take less than 30 minutes to resolve.

**What's included in the takedown?**
Everything we installed comes down — strands, stakes, clips, and any temporary anchors. We schedule takedowns from January 2 through January 31. You pick the window that works and we handle the rest. Your yard will be back to how we found it.

**How does the deposit work?**
A deposit is required to hold your install window. The amount varies by package size and is applied to your total balance. The remaining balance is collected on install day before we leave. Deposits are non-refundable within [14] days of your scheduled date, but we can reschedule at no charge with at least [7] days' notice.

**Do you do commercial properties?**
Yes. Storefronts, office entries, apartment buildings, and retail properties. Commercial jobs are quoted on a walkthrough. Reach out early — commercial installs have a longer lead time and availability is more limited.

**What areas do you serve?**
We serve Austin and communities within approximately [30 miles], including Cedar Park, Round Rock, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander, Kyle, Buda, Lakeway, and Bee Cave. Request a quote if you're not sure whether we cover your location.

**What happens if we get a late-season storm?**
Texas weather is unpredictable. If severe weather damages a section after install, contact us and we'll assess. Storm repair is handled case by case. Cosmetic issues from wind are usually covered under mid-season service. Structural damage to your home or landscaping is not within our scope.

**Can I book the same package next year?**
Yes, and returning clients get early access to the booking calendar before it opens to the public. We'll reach out in the summer to confirm you want to rebook.
