The Complete Guide to Solar Tax Incentives in Austin (2024)

As residents of Austin increasingly look towards sustainable energy, solar panels have become a prominent feature across local rooftops. Beyond the environmental benefits, the financial incentives available in 2024 make the transition to solar more accessible than ever for Travis County homeowners.

Federal Solar Investment Tax Credit (ITC)

The most significant incentive currently available is the Federal Solar Investment Tax Credit. For systems installed in 2024, homeowners can claim a 30% tax credit on the total cost of their solar panel system. This credit is applied directly to your federal income taxes, significantly reducing the upfront burden of installation.

Austin Energy Rebates

Locally, Austin Energy continues to offer robust rebates. Residents who complete the solar education course and use a participating contractor can qualify for a capacity-based incentive. Currently, this translates to $2,500 for eligible residential installations, further sweetening the deal on top of federal credits.

Property Tax Exemptions in Texas

One of the hidden financial perks of installing solar in Texas is the property tax exemption. Under the Texas Property Tax Code, the increase in home value resulting from a solar installation is 100% exempt from property taxes. You get the resale value boost without the recurring tax penalty.
