Costs & Incentives

What Does Solar Actually
Cost in Austin?

The average residential solar install in Austin runs between $18,000 and $28,000 before incentives. That's a wide range because it depends on your roof size, the panels you choose, and how much energy you use.

The good news: incentives bring that number down significantly.

30%

Federal Tax Credit

The federal Investment Tax Credit lets you deduct 30% of your total solar cost from your federal taxes. On a $22,000 system, that's $6,600 back.

$$$

Austin Energy Rebate

Austin Energy offers a rebate for residential solar installations. The amount varies by year and system size — check current rates on their site.

$0

Property Tax Increase

Solar panels increase your home's value, but Texas law says that increase is exempt from property taxes. Your home is worth more. Your tax bill stays the same.

Realistic Payback

Most Austin homeowners break even in 7–9 years.

After that, it's essentially free electricity for the remaining 15–20 years of your panel warranty.
