### Voice & Tone

- Tone: warm, plain, and direct. Sounds like a trusted neighbor, not a salesperson.
- Tone: honest about numbers. Gives real cost ranges and payback times, and says when results vary.
- Vocabulary: conversational and concrete. Uses short words like roof, bill, storm, and hail. Explains solar and insurance terms in plain language.
- Personality: Texas-rooted and community minded. Faith-informed integrity. Full-service and calm under pressure.
- Personality: educator first. Frames every claim as helping the homeowner decide, not pushing a sale.
- Do: lead with the number the homeowner asks about, then explain what drives it.
- Do: use 'we' and 'you'. Speak to one homeowner at a time.
- Do: name the local context. Say Texas, Central Texas, Austin, and the county.
- Do: say 'no pressure', 'free estimate', and 'no commitment' when inviting contact.
- Don't: mention the federal solar tax credit. It expired December 31, 2025.
- Don't: use hype words like revolutionary, unbeatable, or limited-time.
- Don't: hide caveats. Always say numbers vary by roof, usage, utility, and carrier.
- Don't: use jargon without a plain explanation right after it.

### Usage Guide

#### Colors

This is a light theme. Use Page Background #ffffff for the page, Warm Paper Surface #f9f7f4 or Cool Mist Surface #f8fafc for cards and alternating bands, and Primary Text #000000 or Midnight Slate Text #0f172a for headings and body copy, with Steel Muted Text #62748e for captions and helper text. Reserve Deep Sky Section #082f43 and Night Sky Section #040a17 for dark feature bands such as the calculator or hero, paired with Golden Sun Eyebrow #ffd06b for small labels. Use Texas Flag Red #de1017 for the logo and primary calls to action, Sunset Orange #ea580c for warm gradient accents, Savings Green #00bb7f for positive savings figures, and Alert Red #fb2c36 only for errors.

#### Fonts

Set headlines in Gloock, a high-contrast serif that gives the brand its editorial, trustworthy feel, and use BespokeSerif-Variable for large display statements and pull quotes. Set all body copy, buttons, and labels in Switzer-Variable for a clean, modern read, falling back to Helvetica when it is not available. Use JetBrains Mono for numbers in the savings calculator, stat tiles, and eyebrow labels so figures line up and feel precise.

#### Border Radii

Use Button .75rem for buttons and inputs, and Card 1rem for standard content cards and guide tiles. Step up to Large Card 1.5rem for feature cards and Section Panel 2.25rem for full-width bands like the calculator or consultation block. Use Pill 999px for tags, badges, and the eyebrow labels, and Circle 50% for avatars and icon dots.

#### Spacing

Build on a 4px grid. Use Tight .5rem between related items such as an icon and its label, Base 1rem for gaps inside cards and between paragraphs, and Relaxed 1.5rem for card padding. Use Spacious 2rem between groups of content inside a section, and Section 24px to Section Gap 28px for padding around stat tiles and between major blocks.
